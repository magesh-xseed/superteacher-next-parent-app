01 First launch

Splash and onboarding

PurposeIntroduce the app’s three features, then lead the parent to log in or sign up.

02 Account

Sign in or sign up

PurposeSign in fast with a mobile OTP (SMS or WhatsApp), a password or email, or create an account.

03 Home tab

My Child

PurposeOne home screen: this month’s badge goals, this week’s homework and practice, this month’s home activities, and teacher messages.

04 Feature 1

Homework

PurposeFollow homework set by the teacher, ask for help when the child is stuck, and record when it’s done.

05 Feature 2

Weekly Practice

PurposePractise this week’s class topics at home: a short video, then questions with instant feedback.

06 Feature 3 · school subscribed

Learnometer

PurposeShow the child’s latest assessment results, and monthly home activities that work on the gaps it found.

07 Feature 3 · school not subscribed

Learnometer explainer

PurposeWhen the school hasn’t subscribed, explain Learnometer instead of showing an empty tab.
